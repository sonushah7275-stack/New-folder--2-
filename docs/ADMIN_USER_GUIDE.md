# TEJOVA — Administrator User Guide

Welcome to the **TEJOVA Admin Console**! This step-by-step user manual guides non-technical administrative staff through managing journal articles, products, categories, wellness pillars, customer form submissions, and general website settings.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Console

1. Open your web browser and navigate to:  
   **[https://tejova-admin-dashboard.onrender.com/](https://tejova-admin-dashboard.onrender.com/)**
2. Enter your authorized administrator email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In to Admin Console**.
4. Upon successful login, you will be redirected to the main Dashboard overview.

---

## 2. Managing Journal Articles (`/journals`)

The **Journal** module allows you to publish stories, wellness research, and lifestyle articles.

### 2.1 Creating a New Article
1. Click **Journal** in the left sidebar menu.
2. Click the **+ Add Article** button in the top right.
3. Fill in the article details:
   - **Title**: Enter the headline.
   - **Slug**: Auto-generated or custom URL friendly handle.
   - **Category**: Select the category (e.g. *Mindfulness*, *Nourishment*, *Longevity*).
   - **Author**: Enter author name.
   - **Cover Image**: Click **Upload Cover Image** to upload a high-resolution banner image (JPG, PNG, WEBP max 5MB).
   - **Article Font Style**: Select default typography theme.

### 2.2 Formatting Article Content with the Word-like Editor
Use the toolbar at the top of the **Article Content** box to format text:
- **Selected-Text Font Style**: Highlight specific words/sentences and select a typography style from the **Font Style** dropdown (*Serif Old Style*, *Technology Variable*, *Feeling Vintage*, *Feeling Sincere*, *Feeling Rugged*, *DM Sans Regular*).
- **Font Size**: Select text and pick a font size from `10px` to `48px`.
- **Highlight Box**: Highlight text and click **Highlight Box**. Open **Box Color ▾** to select background colors (*Cream*, *Gold*, *Copper*, *Sage*, *Deep Green*, *Midnight Blue*).
- **Horizontal Divider Line**: Click **Divider** to insert a line. Click **Line Color ▾** to pick line accent colors.
- **Line Height**: Select paragraph spacing (`1.0`, `1.15`, `1.25`, `1.5`, `1.75`, `2.0`).
- **Inline Images**: Click the **Image** icon to upload images directly into the article body with optional captions.
4. Set status to **Published** or **Draft**, then click **Save Article**.

---

## 3. Managing Products Catalog (`/products`)

1. Click **Products** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Product**.
3. Complete product fields:
   - **Product Name** & **SKU Code**.
   - **Price** & optional **Compare At Price** (for sales/discount display).
   - **Stock Inventory Count**.
   - **Cover Image Upload**: Select product image file.
4. Click **Save Product**.

---

## 4. Managing Pillars, Categories, & Inquiries

- **Pillars** (`/pillars`): Upload cover images and edit description text for *Vitality*, *Nourishment*, *Lifestyle*, and *Longevity* homepage cards.
- **Contact Inquiries** (`/contacts`): Review messages sent by customers through the website contact form.
- **Subscribers** (`/subscribers`): Download or view customer newsletter email subscriptions.
- **Site Settings** (`/settings`): Update business hours, support email address, contact phone number, and social media links.
